# IISc Alumni Store — Returns & Shipping Policy

## Returns

Items can be returned within 30 days of purchase, provided they are in
original packaging with tags attached. Personalized or engraved items
(nameplates, custom convocation frames) are final sale and cannot be
returned unless defective.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business
days of the returned item being received and inspected at the campus
store.

## Shipping

Orders above ₹999 (subtotal, before discounts) ship free within India.
Orders below that threshold carry a flat ₹49 shipping fee. Delivery
typically takes 4-7 business days within India; international shipping is
not currently offered.

## Damaged or incorrect items

Report a damaged or incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery, with
photos of the item and packaging, to qualify for a free replacement or
full refund without needing to return the original item.
