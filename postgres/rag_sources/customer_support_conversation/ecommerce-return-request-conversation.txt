Customer contacted support wanting to return a pair of wireless
headphones purchased two weeks earlier, reporting one earcup had stopped
producing sound. Support confirmed the order fell within the return
window, apologized for the inconvenience, and explained that since the
item appeared defective rather than simply unwanted, it qualified for a
full refund without a restocking fee. Support emailed a prepaid return
shipping label and advised the refund would post within 5-7 business
days of the warehouse receiving the item. Customer confirmed receipt of
the label and ended the conversation.
